Pain Management

Iontopherisis Patch

A non-invasive therapy that uses a gentle electrical current to deliver anti-inflammatory medication through the skin directly to the affected area. Helps reduce pain, swelling, and inflammation—perfect for managing chronic pain conditions and improving mobility without drugs.

  • ✓ Non-invasive transdermal drug delivery
  • ✓ Reduce pain, swelling, and inflammation
  • ✓ Improve mobility without oral drugs
